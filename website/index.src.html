The problem

A great take.
A forgettable video.

Weeks of practice, one perfect run, filmed on a phone on a stand. Then you have three choices, and none of them was made for you.

01

Post it as filmed

Honest, and it sounds like you. But it looks like every other phone on a stand, the room is lit by a ceiling lamp, and the phone microphone flattens the piano. People scroll on.

Looks flat. Sounds small.
02

Learn a video editor

Premiere, After Effects, particle plugins, keyframes. It can look amazing, after weeks of learning and hours per video, because the editor has no idea which note you played when. Every spark is placed by hand.

Takes weeks. Then hours, every time.
03

Use a MIDI visualizer

Beautiful falling notes, built around the MIDI file. Your footage, your room and your sound come second: many draw a keyboard of their own, and the camera, the light and the audio are still yours to fix elsewhere.

The idea

A video editor sees frames.
Pianoverse hears notes.

Give it the MIDI from your digital piano, or just the video: it listens and finds the notes itself. From then on every effect knows what you played, when, and how hard.

How it works

One take.
One sitting.

Five steps, left to right, the same every time. No timeline to learn, no keyframes, no plugins. Most of it is choosing what you like.

  1. 01ImportDrop in the video from your phone. Add the MIDI file if your piano records one.
  2. 02AudioPianoverse finds the notes, lines them up with the video and sets up the sound.
  3. 03CalibrateMark the two ends of your keyboard. It follows the keys from there.
  4. 04StylePick a look, a light and a camera. Tune anything you like on the timeline.
  5. 05ExportShorts, landscape or square, encoded on your graphics card.

Questions

Before you
download.

My piano has no MIDI. Does it still work?

Yes. Pianoverse listens to the video and finds the notes itself. MIDI just makes it exact.

I filmed on an iPhone. Will it open?

Yes, including the HEVC videos iPhones record by default.

My phone moved a little while I played.

That's fine. The keyboard is followed frame by frame, so the notes stay on the keys.

Is there a Mac version?

Not yet. Pianoverse is Windows only for now.

What does it cost?

Nothing. Pianoverse is free, with no login and no sign-up.

Can I post the videos anywhere?

Yes, they're yours. The grand piano sound is free to use with a credit line.
